hey, i'm aditya

i'm a founder, researcher, and content creator from pune, india. i like building things fast, learning in public, and finding people who push me.

what i've been up to:

  • co-founded aozumi, a cloud that deploys ai-built apps and agents, puts them to sleep when idle, and shares them through revocable links. 1,200+ beta users in 3 weeks.
  • co-founded astrail with riza: open-source tooling that turns apis into typed tools for ai agents. we interviewed at yc s26 and with z fellows.
  • got into uc berkeley '30 for ieor. couldn't join because the student visa slot didn't come through in time.
  • published research on self-healing materials for long-duration space missions in the iyrc journal, and won stanford's best research review award.
  • picked as 1 of 11 residents (from 20,000+ applicants) for nas daily's ai residency in dubai. shipped an ai readiness test that hit 15,000 users in 24 hours.
  • run content strategy for whyverse official and marco learns: 177M+ views on stem, innovation, sustainability, and philosophy.
  • won the un association global innovation sdg challenge and an indian government award with action for world.
  • 2× gold honour at iymc & iaac, and 5× international hackathon winner.

btw, if you're building something, figuring stuff out, or just curious about tech, i'm always down for a convo. dms are open on x and linkedin.
